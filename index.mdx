## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get it running">
    Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart) — clone, `pnpm install`, `pnpm dev`, then open the demo. Five minutes, fully local, with a fictional student's data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the mental model">
    Read [Architecture](/amazecc/02-architecture/README) to understand the one-page shell, the sync engine, and the atoms-vs-cache state split.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first change">
    Work through [Getting Started](/amazecc/01-getting-started/README) — the full first-hour tour from clone to your first merged PR.
  </Step>
</Steps>
